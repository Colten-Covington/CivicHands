# Product foundation

## Service lanes

1. **Public care:** litter, small debris, mowing, brush, and other low-risk work on shared spaces.
2. **City referral:** active traffic hazards, utility issues, large or suspended limbs, hazardous waste, drainage infrastructure, and work requiring permits or trained crews.
3. **Neighbor support:** opt-in rides, yard work, cleaning, wellness check-ins, and other assistance where identity, consent, privacy, safeguarding, and insurance matter.

## Recognition model

CivicHands should recognize dependable helpers without turning service into a game. A profile may show completed categories, approximate service history, references, organization affiliation, and verification indicators. It should not show points, ranks, streak pressure, public failure counts, or a citywide leaderboard.

## Safety boundaries for the pilot

- Never encourage a volunteer to enter an active roadway or handle utilities, hazardous materials, large trees, weapons, or suspected crime evidence.
- Hide exact residential addresses until the requester consents to a specific helper.
- Treat rides, in-home help, minors, and vulnerable adults as later controlled programs rather than open marketplace tasks.
- Make completion reversible and reviewable; a photo alone does not prove consent or quality.
- Preserve an audit trail of status changes and referrals.

## Initial entities

- User and profile
- Organization and membership
- Need report
- Claim
- Completion update
- City referral
- Consent record
- Safety/moderation report
- Community reference
